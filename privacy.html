Privacy Policy

Tamar Lane Poster · Last updated: November 19, 2026

1. Who we are

Tamar Lane Poster is a private tool used by the author Tamar Lane to post her own book content to her own social media accounts.

2. What information is collected

When the author connects her TikTok account, the app receives only:

  • basic profile information (such as account name and profile picture)
  • a secure access key from TikTok that allows posting to that account

The app does not collect information from the public, viewers, followers or other TikTok users.

3. How the information is used

This information is used only to publish the author's own posts to her own TikTok account and to confirm that each post went through. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

4. Sharing

The information is never sold or rented. It is handled by the secure connection service that links the app to TikTok (Composio), only to make posting work.

5. Storage and security

Access keys are stored securely by the connection service and are used only while posting.

6. Deleting your data

Access can be removed at any time from the TikTok app (Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions). To ask for any stored information to be deleted, send a message on Instagram to @jrahoj.

7. Children

This app is not intended for children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.

9. Contact

Questions about privacy: send a message on Instagram to @jrahoj.
